## Spec

Part 3.4 follow-up adds `services/risk-shield/wallclock.py`, a harness that waits for a wall-clock target in sleeps of at most 60 s and re-reads the clock after each one. `run_scheduler` and `run_news_poller` wait only through it. After a Mac sleep, each loop notices the real time within 60 s of the Mac waking, and the scheduler logs `Missed N health check slot(s)` then, even when it wakes between slots.

It must **not**:
- change slot times, the 60 s grace, the 16:20 ET settle, the 15-min poll cadence, `poll_once`, or `run_check` beyond addition 1's `pausedSeconds`
- reorder or remove a health payload key, or change the publish throttle
- catch up a missed slot, or run a check outside its grace on wake
- change the restart rule (decisions 2026-09-10): a loop that has handled nothing yet warns only for the slot it lands in
- touch `ratelimit.py`, the inputs endpoint's 60 s reuse, or any `asyncio.wait_for` timeout (decision 6)
- add a dependency, setting, env var or compose change
- build 3.6b's loop (3.6b uses the harness; decision 5)
- touch prod except the G15 item

Acceptance:
- **Tests:** every failure-branch row names a passing test in `tf-risk-shield-dev`. The 546 existing tests still pass, except the 8 tests updated in place (7 loop tests and the payload shape test, named in the tables).
- **No direct sleep in a loop:** `grep -rn "sleep(" services/risk-shield --include="*.py"` outside `tests/` shows only `wallclock.py` and `ratelimit.py`.

## Decisions

### 1. The harness: `wallclock.py` (**your call**)

- `MAX_SLEEP_SECONDS = 60`
- `wait_seconds(target, now) -> float`: `min(target − now, 60)`, never below 0. Pure. A naive `target` or `now` raises `ValueError`.
- `async sleep_until(target, *, clock, sleep=asyncio.sleep, process_clock=time.monotonic, log=logger) -> Wake`: loops `sleep(wait_seconds(target, clock()))` until `clock() ≥ target`, then returns `Wake(now, paused_seconds)` with that reading (addition 1 adds `paused_seconds`, `process_clock` and `log`; decision 10). It returns without sleeping when the target has passed. Cancellation propagates.
- **Why a module, not an inline `min(delay, 60)` in each loop:** 3.6b adds a third loop, and a waiting queue needs `wait_seconds` on its own (decision 5). One cap, one pin test.
- **Alternative:** inline caps in both loops, with 3.6b copying the pattern. Less code now (~10 lines), but three copies of the rule.

### 2. Why 60 s

- **The cause:** `asyncio.sleep` runs on the Docker VM's monotonic clock, which doesn't advance while macOS sleeps. A sleep of ≤ 60 s ends at most 60 s after the Mac wakes, and the loop then sees the real time.
- **Cost:** ≤ 1,440 wakes a day per loop. Each chunk compares two datetimes; the XNYS calendar is read once per wait, not per chunk.
- **Accepted edge:** a wake inside a slot's first minute can still land past the 60 s grace, and that slot is skipped with the WARNING. A shorter cap narrows this at 6× the wakes for 10 s.

### 3. The scheduler

- **The wait:** `await wallclock.sleep_until(target, clock=clock, sleep=sleep)`, where `target` is `next_slot_after(now)`. On a loop error it is `now + FALLBACK_SLEEP_SECONDS` (300 s, unchanged), so a persistent error still logs once per 300 s.
- **The due branch is unchanged:** a slot whose window holds `now` runs within grace, or is reported with `_missed_between(last_start, start) + 1`.
- **New wake check** (the behaviour change): when no slot is due and `last_start` is set, read `last_slot_before(now)`. If that slot started after `last_start`:
  - log **one** WARNING, `Missed N health check slot(s) up to <slot start> (woke Ns after that slot)`, where N = `_missed_between(last_start, prev) + 1`
  - set `last_start` to that slot, so it isn't reported twice
- **The case it fixes:** last check Fri 09:35, the Mac sleeps 09:36 → Sat 01:36. Today that wake is silent. After this part it logs `Missed 78 … up to 2026-09-11T20:20:00+00:00` (09:40…16:00 = 77, plus the settle).
- **Boot:** `last_start` is None, so nothing is reported beyond the landing slot. That's the restart rule, unchanged.

### 4. The news poller

- `now = await wallclock.sleep_until(target, clock=clock, sleep=sleep)` replaces `sleep(delay)` + `now = clock()`. The early-wake branch moves into the harness.
- **Unchanged:** the late-wake WARNING (≥ 900 s after its slot), one poll now, no catch-up, the calendar warning once per ET day.
- **Loop error:** the 900 s fallback waits through the harness.

### 5. What 3.6b gets

- `run_brief_loop` waits through `wallclock`. Its "next slot or a queued regime request" wait is `asyncio.wait_for(queue.get(), timeout=wallclock.wait_seconds(target, clock()))` in a loop, re-reading the clock after each chunk. `asyncio.wait_for` runs on the same stopped clock, so the same cap applies.
- `docs/specs/3.6b.md` v2 carries the change, its test assertions and the re-cut band.

### 6. Other timers that stop with the Mac (checked, not changed)

| where | clock | after a Mac sleep | why it stays |
|---|---|---|---|
| `main.py` inputs reuse (`time.monotonic`, 60 s) | monotonic | a document assembled just before the sleep can be served for up to 60 s after wake | the endpoint only; 3.6b's generation never reads the reuse. Carried forward |
| `ratelimit.py` limiter | monotonic | a window across the sleep waits longer, never shorter | fails safe |
| `cache.py` memory cooldowns | `time.time()` | none: wall clock | n/a |
| `asyncio.wait_for` on ingest, FRED, Finnhub and startup | monotonic | a call in flight stretches its timeout by the sleep, and ends within the remaining timeout after wake | bounded |

### 10. Addition 1: host pause (after approval)

- **The WARNING:** after each chunk the harness compares wall-clock elapsed with process-clock elapsed (`time.monotonic`). A gap over `PAUSE_THRESHOLD_SECONDS = 120` logs one WARNING, `host paused ~Xh Ym (wall +A s, process +B s)`.
  - X h Y m is the gap rounded to the minute; A and B are that chunk's wall and process seconds.
  - It logs on the caller's logger (`log=`), so the line names `scheduler` or `news_poller`. One pause gives one line per loop.
  - The loop's `Missed` line is logged after the harness returns, so the pause line always comes first.
  - A wall clock stepped forward by more than 120 s (NTP) also reads as a pause. Accepted: the clock did jump.
- **`Wake(now, paused_seconds)`:** the sum of that wait's gaps over the threshold, rounded to whole seconds, or None.
- **`pausedSeconds` on the health payload:**
  - The scheduler adds each wake's `paused_seconds` to `state.pending_paused_seconds`.
  - The next `run_check` that reaches the publish step passes it to `publish_health`, then clears it, published or not. A check skipped by the quotes lock doesn't consume it.
  - Every other payload carries `pausedSeconds: null`.
  - **The throttle is unchanged:** if that first check doesn't publish (no regime change, no 10-point move), the value appears in no payload. The WARNING still has it.
- **`PAYLOAD_KEYS` is append-only:** `pausedSeconds` goes last, and the 14 existing keys keep their order. `NEWS_KEYS` was "the last three keys", so it becomes the fixed slice `PAYLOAD_KEYS[11:14]`. Nothing outside risk-shield reads the payload yet (Phase 5).
- **The news poller** logs the WARNING and ignores `paused_seconds`.
